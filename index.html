Linear to rotary

The piston can only slide. Its wrist pin is joined by the connecting rod to a crank pin set off-centre on the shaft. When the gas pushes the piston down, the rod pushes the crank pin around the shaft, so the force becomes torque. The heavy flywheel stores that energy and carries the shaft through the compression half, pushing the piston back up.

x(θ) = r(1 − cos θ) + L − √(L² − r² sin² θ)

Reading the cycle

Top dead centre (θ = 0°) is state 1, the smallest volume. Bottom dead centre (θ = 180°) is state 3, the largest. The coloured ring around the flywheel marks which process each part of the turn belongs to. The area enclosed on the P–V diagram is the net work delivered to the shaft each revolution: W = QH − QC = 3277 J − 1966 J ≈ 1311 J.

η = 1 − TC/TH = 1 − 300/500 = 40%
